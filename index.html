Lunch Protocol

Midday High Protein

Option A Ingredients: 569 kcal • 31.8g Protein
Fresh Paneer (Raw) 150 g
Full-Fat Curd 150 g
Pure Desi Ghee 1 level tsp (5g)
Instructions:

Heat Ghee, sauté onion/tomato/spices, add crumbled Paneer, cook for 3 mins. Serve with chilled Curd.

Option B Ingredients: 568 kcal • 33.4g Protein
Soya Chunks (Dry) 50 g
Full-Fat Curd 200 g
Pure Desi Ghee 2 level tsp (10g)
Instructions:

Boil & squeeze Soya Chunks. Sauté in Ghee with masala & ginger-garlic. Toss 4 mins, squeeze lemon & serve with Curd.

Option C Ingredients: 525 kcal • 24.5g Protein
Kala Chana (Raw/Dry) 60 g
Full-Fat Curd 150 g
Pure Desi Ghee 1 level tsp (5g)
Instructions:

Soak & pressure cook Kala Chana. Prepare Ghee tadka with cumin & tomato masala. Simmer 5 mins & squeeze lemon.

Dinner Protocol

Evening Meal

Option A Ingredients: 446 kcal • 30.1g Protein
Firm Tofu (Raw) 180 g
Hummus Dip 50 g
Pure Desi Ghee 1 level tsp (5g)
Instructions:

Sauté Tofu in Ghee until golden. Serve with Hummus dip.

Option B Ingredients: 466 kcal • 39.5g Protein
Firm Tofu (Raw Cubes) 200 g
Chana Daal (Dry) 45 g
Pure Desi Ghee 1 level tsp (5g)
Instructions:

Boil Daal. Prepare Ghee tadka base, add Tofu & cooked Daal, simmer 5 mins.
