Oats, Full-Fat Curd & Peanut Butter Shake

Breakfast • Fast Energy & High Satiety

533 kcal 21.7g Protein

Exact Raw Ingredients

Rolled Oats (Dry) 40 g
Full-Fat Curd (Whole Dahi) 250 g
Natural Peanut Butter 35 g

Easy Preparation Steps

  1. 1 Weigh dry Rolled Oats and add to blender jar.
  2. 2 Add weighed Full-Fat Curd, Peanut Butter, and 150ml chilled water.
  3. 3 Blend for 60 seconds until creamy and enjoy immediately.

Lunch Protocol

Midday Fuel • Sautéed in Pure Desi Ghee

Option A Ingredients

569 kcal • 31.8g Protein
Fresh Paneer (Raw) 150 g
Full-Fat Curd (Side Bowl) 150 g
Pure Desi Ghee 1 level tsp (5g)

Cooking Steps (Paneer Bhurji)

  1. 1 Heat 1 level tsp Desi Ghee in a pan and sauté onion, tomato, and spices.
  2. 2 Add crumbled Paneer and cook for 3 minutes.
  3. 3 Serve hot alongside chilled Full-Fat Curd.

Option B Ingredients

568 kcal • 33.4g Protein
Soya Chunks (Dry) 50 g
Full-Fat Curd (Side Bowl) 200 g
Pure Desi Ghee 2 level tsp (10g)

Cooking Steps (Soya Chunks Fry)

  1. 1 Boil Soya Chunks in salted water for 5 mins, rinse with cold water & squeeze well.
  2. 2 Heat 2 level tsp Desi Ghee; sauté onion, ginger-garlic, tomato, and garam masala.
  3. 3 Toss in soya chunks, pan-fry for 4 mins, squeeze fresh lemon juice, and serve with Curd.

Dinner Protocol

Evening Meal • Flexible Choice

Option A Ingredients

446 kcal • 30.1g Protein
Firm Tofu (Raw) 180 g
Hummus Dip 50 g
Pure Desi Ghee 1 level tsp (5g)

Cooking Steps (Stir-Fry)

  1. 1 Melt 1 level tsp Desi Ghee in a pan over medium heat.
  2. 2 Add cubed Firm Tofu and capsicum, stir-frying until golden brown.
  3. 3 Serve warm alongside Hummus dip.

Option B Ingredients

466 kcal • 39.5g Protein
Firm Tofu (Raw Cubes) 200 g
Chana Daal (Raw/Dry) 45 g
Pure Desi Ghee 1 level tsp (5g)

Cooking Steps (Tadka Curry)

  1. 1 Boil weighed Chana Daal until soft.
  2. 2 Heat 1 level tsp Desi Ghee in a pot, tempering cumin seeds and tomato curry spices.
  3. 3 Add cooked daal and Tofu cubes; simmer for 5 minutes.
