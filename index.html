Oats, Full-Fat Curd & PB Shake

Breakfast • High Satiety Protocol

620 kcal
Scaled Ingredients & Calories:
Rolled Oats (Dry)
55 g 206 kcal
Full-Fat Curd (Dahi)
250 g 170 kcal
Natural Peanut Butter
40 g 244 kcal
Preparation:

Add dry oats, full-fat curd, peanut butter, and 200ml chilled water into a blender. Blend high for 60 seconds until creamy and serve immediately.

Lunch Protocol

Midday High Protein Fuel

Option A Ingredients: 660 kcal total
Fresh Paneer (Raw)
180 g 476 kcal
Full-Fat Curd
150 g 102 kcal
Pure Desi Ghee
1.2 tsp (6g) 54 kcal
Onion, Tomato & Spice Base 28 kcal
Instructions:

Melt Ghee in a pan. Sauté onion, tomato, and green chili with spices. Add crumbled Paneer and cook for 3 minutes. Serve with chilled Full-Fat Curd.

Option B Ingredients: 660 kcal total
Soya Chunks (Dry)
70 g 242 kcal
Full-Fat Curd
200 g 136 kcal
Pure Desi Ghee
2.4 tsp (12g) 108 kcal
Onion, Tomato & Spice Base 74 kcal
Instructions:

Boil Soya Chunks for 5 mins, drain, and squeeze excess water. Sauté in Ghee with ginger-garlic and spices for 4 minutes. Squeeze lemon juice & serve with Curd.

Option C Ingredients: 660 kcal total
Kala Chana (Raw/Dry)
100 g 378 kcal
Full-Fat Curd
150 g 102 kcal
Pure Desi Ghee
2.4 tsp (12g) 108 kcal
Onion, Tomato & Spice Base 72 kcal
Instructions:

Soak Kala Chana overnight & pressure cook soft. Prepare Ghee tadka with cumin, tomatoes, and garam masala. Toss cooked chana 5 mins & finish with lemon juice.
