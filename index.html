Oats, Curd & Peanut Butter Power Shake

Breakfast • Quick High-Energy Start

560 kcal 28g Protein

Exact Raw Ingredients

Rolled Oats (Dry) 40 g
Plain Low-Fat Curd (Dahi) 250 g
Natural Peanut Butter 35 g
Water / Chilled Ice As needed

Easy Preparation Steps

  1. 1 Weigh Rolled Oats dry on a kitchen scale and add to blender jar.
  2. 2 Add weighed Curd, Peanut Butter, and 150ml chilled water or ice cubes.
  3. 3 Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until completely smooth. Drink immediately.
Pro Tip: Add a pinch of cinnamon or a drop of stevia for extra flavor without extra calories.

Spiced Paneer & Curd Bhurji Bowl

Lunch • High Protein & Sustained Satiety

585 kcal 34g Protein

Exact Raw Ingredients

Fresh Paneer (Raw) 150 g
Plain Curd (Side Bowl) 150 g
Onion, Tomato, Green Chili Free Veggies
Turmeric, Cumin, Garam Masala To Taste

Easy Preparation Steps

  1. 1 Chop onion, tomato, and green chili. Crumble the weighed Paneer.
  2. 2 Sauté veggies in a non-stick pan with a splash of water and spices until soft.
  3. 3 Add crumbled Paneer, mix well for 3 minutes. Serve hot alongside your chilled Curd.
Cooking Tip: Use non-stick cookware to cook without added oil, preserving your exact macros.

Dinner Protocol

Evening Meal • Flexible High-Protein Choice

Option A Raw Ingredients

570 kcal • 33g Protein
Firm Tofu (Raw) 180 g
Hummus Dip 50 g
Mixed Capsicum & Onion Free Veggies

Preparation Steps (Stir-Fry)

  1. 1 Cube Firm Tofu into bitesize pieces. Sauté with capsicum and onion in a hot non-stick pan.
  2. 2 Season with black pepper, oregano, chili flakes, and salt. Cook until edges turn golden brown.
  3. 3 Serve warm with weighed Hummus as a savory dip.

Option B Raw Ingredients

570 kcal • 33g Protein
Firm Tofu (Raw Cubes) 140 g
Chana Daal (Raw/Dry) 30 g
Tomato & Indian Curry Spices Free Ingredients

Preparation Steps (Curry)

  1. 1 Boil weighed Chana Daal until cooked soft.
  2. 2 Prepare a light tomato masala curry base in a pot with cumin and spices.
  3. 3 Add cooked daal and Tofu cubes. Simmer together for 5 minutes. Enjoy hot.
