Oats, Full-Fat Curd & PB Shake

Breakfast • Fast Satiety

533 kcal
Scaled Ingredients & Calories:
Rolled Oats (Dry)
40 g 150 kcal
Full-Fat Curd (Dahi)
250 g 170 kcal
Natural Peanut Butter
35 g 213 kcal
Preparation:

Add dry oats, full-fat curd, peanut butter, and 150ml chilled water into a blender. Blend high for 60 seconds until creamy and serve immediately.

Lunch Protocol

Midday High Protein

Option A Ingredients: 569 kcal total • 31.8g Protein
Fresh Paneer (Raw)
150 g 397 kcal
Full-Fat Curd
150 g 102 kcal
Pure Desi Ghee

Instructions:

Melt 1 tsp Ghee in a pan. Sauté onion, tomato, and green chili with spices. Add crumbled Paneer and cook for 3 minutes. Serve with chilled Full-Fat Curd.

Option B Ingredients: 568 kcal total • 33.4g Protein
Soya Chunks (Dry)
50 g 173 kcal
Full-Fat Curd
200 g 136 kcal
Pure Desi Ghee
2 level tsp (10g) 90 kcal
Instructions:

Boil Soya Chunks for 5 mins, drain, and squeeze excess water. Sauté in 2 tsp Ghee with ginger-garlic and spices for 4 minutes. Squeeze fresh lemon juice & serve with Curd.

Option C Ingredients: 525 kcal total • 24.5g Protein

60 g 220 kcal
Full-Fat Curd
150 g 102 kcal
Pure Desi Ghee
1 level tsp (5g) 45 kcal
Instructions:

Soak and pressure cook Kala Chana soft. Prepare Ghee tadka with cumin, tomatoes, and garam masala. Toss cooked chana for 5 mins and finish with lemon juice.
